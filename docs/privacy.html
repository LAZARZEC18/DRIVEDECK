DRIVEDECK Privacy Policy

Effective 26 September 2026 · App: DRIVEDECK (com.lazarzec.drivedeck) · Developer: Lazar, Perth, Western Australia

DRIVEDECK is a personal Android Auto companion app. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no servers run by the developer. Your data stays on your phone and, if you turn on sync, in a private GitHub repository that you own.

Services the app talks to

  • GitHub (api.github.com): your own private data repository, only if you set up sync.
  • FuelWatch WA (fuelwatch.wa.gov.au): public fuel prices. Your suburb or location area may be sent.
  • OpenStreetMap services: Photon (photon.komoot.io) for place search, Overpass for speed camera and traffic light locations, and OSRM (router.project-osrm.org) for road distance and no-traffic ETA. Search text or coordinates are sent to answer the request.
  • Android's built-in geocoder: turns the start and end of a drive into suburb names. The coordinates are sent to the geocoding service your phone uses (usually Google).
  • Waze, Google Maps, YouTube Music and WhatsApp on your phone, when you open them from DRIVEDECK. Their own privacy policies apply.

None of these services receive your name, contacts or messages from DRIVEDECK.

Sharing and selling

DRIVEDECK does not sell or share personal data, and does not use it for advertising or tracking.

Keeping and deleting data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on the phone. Data in your sync repository is under your control: delete the repository (or the deck.json file) on GitHub to remove it. Revoking the GitHub token stops sync straight away.

Children

DRIVEDECK is made for licensed drivers and is not directed at children.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/LAZARZEC18/DRIVEDECK/issues.
